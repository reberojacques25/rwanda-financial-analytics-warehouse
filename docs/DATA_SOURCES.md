# Data Sources

## Primary Sources

### National Bank of Rwanda (BNR)
- **Interest Rate Structure**: Directly downloadable XLS, 2002-March 2025
  - URL: https://www.bnr.rw/documents/Interest_rate_structure_up_to_March__2025.xls
  - Evidence: VERIFIED_PRIMARY
  - License: Publicly accessible; redistribution unclear

- **Financial Sector Statistics**: Quarterly banking-sector data
  - URL: https://www.bnr.rw/Fsstatistics
  - Evidence: VERIFIED_PRIMARY
  - Format: PDF/Web

- **Monetary Policy and Financial Stability Statement (MPFSS)**
  - URL: https://www.bnr.rw/FinancialStability
  - Evidence: VERIFIED_PRIMARY
  - Contains NPL ratios (2019-2024), banking sector aggregates

- **Exchange Rates**: Daily, monthly, annual averages
  - URL: https://www.bnr.rw/FinancialMarket

### National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR)
- **GDP National Accounts**: Annual and quarterly
  - URL: https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/gross-domestic-product
  - Evidence: VERIFIED_PRIMARY

- **CPI/Inflation**: Monthly publications
  - URL: https://statistics.gov.rw/

## Secondary Sources

### World Bank
- **World Development Indicators - Rwanda**: API accessible
  - URL: https://data.worldbank.org/country/rwanda
  - Evidence: VERIFIED_SECONDARY
  - License: CC BY-4.0
  - API: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/RWA/indicator/{code}

### IMF
- **DataMapper - Rwanda**
  - URL: https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/profile/RWA
  - Evidence: VERIFIED_SECONDARY

## Redistribution Status

- World Bank data: CC BY-4.0 (redistribution allowed with attribution)
- BNR/NISR data: Publicly accessible, redistribution status unclear
- Bank disclosures: Subject to individual bank terms
- Raw data files are NOT committed to the repository
