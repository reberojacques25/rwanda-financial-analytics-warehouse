# Limitations

## Data Coverage

1. **Bank-level granularity**: Public data supports aggregate banking-sector analysis only. A complete bank-by-bank panel for all licensed Rwandan banks is not publicly available.

2. **PDF extraction**: BNR financial sector statistics and NISR GDP are primarily in PDF format. Automated extraction requires careful validation.

3. **Frequency mismatch**: Interest rates are monthly; macro indicators are annual. Analysis must align frequencies explicitly.

4. **Historical gaps**: Some indicators have missing years. These are documented, not interpolated.

## Analytical Limitations

5. **No causal claims**: The data supports descriptive and correlational analysis only.

6. **Macro variables**: National-level indicators (GDP, inflation) are identical for all banks in a period — they cannot explain cross-bank variation.

7. **NPL ratio vs. stock**: BNR publishes NPL ratios more readily than NPL amounts. Do not infer stock from rounded ratios.

8. **Revisions**: GDP and national accounts may be revised or rebased. Always preserve publication version.

## Source Limitations

9. **Secondary sources**: World Bank/IMF data are VERIFIED_SECONDARY. They compile from national sources but may include estimates.

10. **Redistribution**: BNR/NISR redistribution status is unclear. Raw data is not committed to the repository.
